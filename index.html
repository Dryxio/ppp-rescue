    A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

    Old format.
    Simple answers.

    What is a .ppp file?

    PagePlus saves publications such as newsletters, brochures and books as .ppp files.

    Can I open PagePlus files without PagePlus?

    Yes. Choose your file to recover its text and images directly in your browser. No PagePlus installation needed. See file compatibility.

    Can I convert PPP to PDF or Affinity Publisher?

    Download HTML and choose Print → Save as PDF in your browser for a reading copy. You can also reuse the recovered text and images in Affinity Publisher. Exports do not preserve the original page layout or create editable Affinity documents.

    Does my document leave my computer?

    No. Extraction happens in your browser. We do not upload or store your document, and there are no analytics or advertising scripts. Our hosting provider receives normal website request information when you visit. If you choose to send feedback, only the answers you enter are sent through FormSubmit to our inbox; your document is never attached.

    Which PagePlus files can I open?

    PPP Rescue reads ZIP/XML publications, tested with PagePlus X7–X9. When the tool detects missing content, a note appears with your result. View compatibility details ↗

    Is this free?

    Yes. PPP Rescue is a free, open-source community project, released under the MIT license. No signup, subscriptions or AI services.
