    A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

    Old format.
    Simple answers.

    What is a .ppp file?

    A .ppp file can be a publication made with Serif PagePlus, a desktop publishing application. PPP Rescue supports its ZIP/XML document format; other applications also use the same extension.

    Can I open PagePlus files without PagePlus?

    For supported ZIP/XML documents, this tool extracts story text and embedded PNG, JPEG and GIF images without PagePlus. It does not reproduce the original design. The beta has been tested on two X8/X9 documents, so compatibility is still limited.

    Can I convert PPP to PDF or Affinity Publisher?

    This tool exports HTML, plain text and images, not editable Affinity documents. You can open the HTML and use your browser’s Print → Save as PDF for a reflowed reading copy. For the original layout, use PagePlus to export a PDF if you still have access to it.

    Does my document leave my computer?

    No. Extraction happens in your browser. We do not upload or store your document, and there are no analytics or advertising scripts. Our hosting provider receives normal website request information when you visit.

    What might be missing?

    Older binary PPP files, linked images not embedded in the file, vector drawings, WDP images, fonts, special symbols, list numbering and complex formatting are not fully supported. Always compare the recovered content with your original where possible.

    Is this free?

    Yes. PPP Rescue is a free, open-source community project, released under the MIT license. No signup, subscriptions or AI services.
